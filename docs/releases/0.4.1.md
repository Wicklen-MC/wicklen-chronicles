## Follow your next discovery

- Follow a known place or expedition with direction/distance hints, a dotted map route and nearby Waylight sparks.
- Recognize visible registered structures from other mods without revealing hidden or unloaded places.
- Give spirit conversations context from personal journeys, named discoveries and recent outcomes.
- Use a more compact field map and clearer guidance toward the next step.

### Install or update

Minecraft **26.3**, **Fabric Loader 0.20.1+**, Fabric API (tested with
**0.165.0+26.3**) and **Java 25**. Replace the previous Chronicles JAR with
`wicklen-chronicles-0.4.1.jar` on both client and server. The `-sources.jar` is for
developers. Keep world data, personal settings and other mods in place.

Gateway of Doom is optional for courtyard battles. LLM conversations use a
separately configured server-side provider.

This historical release has been superseded by **0.4.2**. Guidance shows a bearing;
it does not expose unexplored terrain or promise a safe path. This is the mod,
not the complete Wicklen modpack.
