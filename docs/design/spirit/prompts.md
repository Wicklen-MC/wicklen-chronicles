Create a landscape concept sheet with a clear 3-column by 2-row grid, six generously spaced numbered panels, numbers 1 through 6. 2: A wandering lantern spirit: a tiny weathered wood-and-copper lantern cage with a living warm light inside; expressive eyes belong to the light, not a pasted face on the cage. Small ribbon-like smoke tail, compact cozy silhouette, no robot limbs.
3: A woodland hood spirit: tiny moss-green leaf hood surrounding a dark recessed face with two warm eyes, uneven leaf hem and a little trailing root/ribbon. Clearly hovering and supernatural, a woodland guide rather than a humanoid gnome; no legs.

Constraints: no huge heads on stick arms, no box-shaped head with rectangular hovering crown, no detailed humanoid hands, no skulls, no horror, no recognizable existing franchise characters, no shiny mobile-game 3D look, no photorealism, no bloom clouds or complex volumetric effects. Communicate glow with a small warm wash around the core, while keeping the underlying buildable geometry visible. The whole sheet should look deliberately exploratory and beautiful, inviting the player to choose a direction.
